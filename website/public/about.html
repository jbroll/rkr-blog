About rkr-blog

rkr-blog is a blog where writing and images work together naturally. Bring in photos from your desktop, Google Drive, OneDrive, or any URL, arrange them in rich layouts, and publish — no markup and no plugins.

Authoring happens entirely in the browser, and it works offline: write, import, and edit without a connection, and your drafts sync automatically when connectivity returns. No ads, no tracking.
