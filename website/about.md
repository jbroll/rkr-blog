# About {{brand.name}}

{{brand.name}} is a blog where writing and images work together naturally.
Bring in photos from your desktop, Google Drive, OneDrive, or any URL,
arrange them in rich layouts, and publish — no markup, no plugins, and no
external services.

Authoring happens entirely in the browser, and it works offline: write,
import, and edit without a connection, and changes sync automatically when
connectivity returns. The whole thing runs on any Node 22 host, so your
content stays on your own server.
